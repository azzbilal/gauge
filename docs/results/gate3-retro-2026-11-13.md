## What the numbers show

1. **Only one criterion separates accepted from rejected tasks.** On `correct_reference`,
   accepted tasks average 4.18 and rejected tasks 1.62. On the other four criteria, accepted
   and rejected tasks are within 0.1 of each other.
   - Of the 13 rejecting reviews, 12 (all from the golden set) fall below the bar on `correct_reference` only. The 13th, a legacy review in CAD tasks v1, falls below the bar on `verifiable` only.
   - 20 of the 24 scores come from the golden set, and its broken tasks are broken in the reference by design (4.5 against 1.5 there). The split mostly reflects how the golden set was built.
2. **Every review written since evidence became required carries evidence.**
   - The 4 of 24 scores without a note are the 2 legacy reviews in CAD tasks v1 and 2 identified reviews in My first campaign.
   - Counting the 3 reviews on IN_REVIEW tasks, 7 reviews have no evidence. All 7 were written on 2026-11-11 or 11-12, before the first evidenced review (2026-11-13 20:02) and the `review_evidence` migration.
   - Coverage does not show quality. Each of the 10 golden-set tasks has exactly one distinct note across its two reviews: the notes are templated, not independent judgements.
3. **No split has happened yet, so adjudication has never run.** All 24 reviews on decided tasks match the final status: 11 accepts on ACCEPTED tasks and 13 rejects on REJECTED ones. The other 3 reviews are accepts on tasks still IN_REVIEW. There is no task-level review decision.
4. **The pipeline works end to end.** Postgres → DuckDB → dbt → tests → snapshot → page runs on one command or one Dagster job, and fails loudly, without leaking credentials, when Postgres is unreachable.

## What the numbers do not show

- **Reviewer calibration is unmeasured.** There are no gold tasks, so the mart has nothing to
  score. Its gold logic is verified only on fixture rows, never on real gold reviews. Null
  here means "unmeasured", not "perfect".
- **Agreement is not a finding.**
  - The golden set's 1.0 holds by construction: its reviews are scripted against author labels.
  - My first campaign's 1.0 rests on a single task.
  - CAD tasks v1 has only legacy reviews, so its agreement is null.
  - Only the golden set reaches the IAA minimum of 10 pairs.
- **The throughput numbers are artifacts.** Tasks per day (10.0, 4.0, 1.5) reflect seeding
  bursts on one or two days, not authoring speed. All three campaigns have a target of 10, and
  only the golden set reaches it.
- **The samples are too small for any rate to carry weight.** There are 13 decided tasks.
  Accept rates of 0.40, 1.00 and 0.50 rest on 10, 1 and 2 decided tasks.
- **There is a data-hygiene wrinkle.** Two different expert rows are both named
  "E2E Weak (Sam Ortiz)". The marts key on expert id, so they stay separate, and the dashboard
  shows an id suffix to tell them apart.
